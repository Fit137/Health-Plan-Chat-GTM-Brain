# Template — agent community and forum post

Campaign A2. Facebook agent groups, Insurance Forums, agent-only Slack and Discord.

**The channel rule that governs everything here: communities reject gated assets and they
reject vendor pitching.** Give the value in the post itself. Disclose the affiliation. A
post that hides the vendor relationship and gets caught costs the channel, not the post.

---

## Structure

**1 · The finding, first line.** No preamble, no "hey everyone". Lead with the thing that
is useful.

**2 · The substance.** Enough that the post stands alone as useful even if nobody clicks
anything. Aggregate data, a checklist, or a specific observation about plan-year changes.

**3 · The method.** How you know. This audience is professionally sceptical and will ask.

**4 · Disclosure.** One line, plain. *"Full disclosure, I build [product] — happy to talk
about it if anyone asks, but this post isn't that."*

**5 · One optional offer, at the bottom, ungated.** The D1 Readiness Scorecard: 90
seconds, no email until the score.

---

## Worked shape

> Ran the same twenty benefit questions against the main line of forty Medicare agencies
> this quarter. Three findings worth sharing:
>
> [Finding 1, with the number.]
> [Finding 2.]
> [Finding 3 — the counter-intuitive one.]
>
> Method: called each line once during office hours and once after, asked plan-specific
> questions from that agency's own published plan list, scored whether a correct answer
> came back.
>
> Full disclosure, I build an AI front line for Medicare agencies. This post isn't a
> pitch — the aggregate is more useful shared than sat on.

---

## Guardrails

- **Minimum sample of 15 agencies before publishing any benchmark.** Thin data destroys
  the only proprietary asset the marketing engine has.
- **No individual agency named without consent.**
- **No carrier or plan named in a way that implies endorsement.**
- **Never gate the value.** D1 gives the score before asking for anything.
- No product claims in the body. If someone asks, answer in comments against
  `reference/objection-handling.md`.
- Run `ops/QA-checklist.md` before posting.
