# Template — landing page

For the website, a driver landing page, or a comparison page. Voice:
`rules/writing-rules.md`. Claims: `reference/value-proposition-icp1.md`.

**Pick the audience before writing a word.** ICP-1 and ICP-2 landing pages share no copy.

---

## Structure

**1 · Headline.** The promise in the reader's terms, not the product's. Under twelve
words.
*Approved:* "The only AI that already knows the plans you sell."

**2 · Subhead.** One sentence naming the specific thing it does. Name a real benefit
category — dental, OTC, copay — never "comprehensive benefit information".

**3 · The demonstration.** This is the page's job. A real question, and a real answer with
its source cited. Not a feature list, not a video, not a testimonial we do not have.

**4 · Three pillars.** Plan Fluency, Answers You Can Stand Behind, Coverage Without the
Headcount. One paragraph each, drawn from `reference/value-proposition-icp1.md`. Do not
rewrite the approved language.

**5 · What it will and will not say.** The escalation behaviour, stated plainly. This
section converts the sceptical reader and it is the one most likely to be cut for length.
Do not cut it.

**6 · Compliance.** TPMO disclaimer, automated-system disclosure, recording and CMS
ten-year retention. Factual, no adjectives.

**7 · Status, if any capability on the page is not shipped.** Say so on the page.

**8 · Price.** Only once published (OF-1). Until then this section does not exist — do not
replace it with "contact us for pricing", which is the demo gate the positioning attacks.

**9 · One call to action.** The Gap Report, or the sandbox. Never "book a demo".

---

## Guardrails

- Every capability on the page carries its status from `rules/feature-status.md`. Nothing
  IN BUILD, ROADMAP or UNVERIFIED appears as though it is live.
- No customer count, logo, testimonial or case study. There are none.
- Every superlative checked against `rules/do-not-say.md`.
- Benefit examples carry the plan year. An undated example is wrong by January.
- No stock photography of smiling seniors. It signals a consumer product and this page
  sells to agencies.
- Run `ops/QA-checklist.md` before publish.
