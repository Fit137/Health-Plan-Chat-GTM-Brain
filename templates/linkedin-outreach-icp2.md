# Template — LinkedIn outreach, ICP-2

Campaign B1. Targeted at Directors of Operations, Compliance Officers and FMO principals.
Method: `reference/outbound-engine.md`. Audience: `reference/value-proposition-icp2.md`.

**Title-verify every prospect before the first touch.** An independent agency owner who
lands in this sequence gets the wrong pitch and is lost — route them to campaign A1
instead.

**Do not pre-run the Gap Report here.** Running it unprompted at ICP-2 scale is not
economic. It is offered, and run on acceptance.

---

## Connection note

Under 300 characters. No pitch. One line of shared context or one aggregate finding.

> Ran answer-quality audits across [N] Medicare agencies this quarter. The downline
> variance was the part that surprised me. Happy to share what came back if it is useful.

## Touch 1 — the aggregate insight

One finding from the aggregate audit data. No attachment. No product.

> Across the agencies audited so far, [specific finding about answer variance or
> after-hours failure]. For a downline your size that is [consequence in their terms].
>
> Would it be useful to see it run across one of your segments?

## Touch 2 — the offer

Offer to run the Gap Report across one downline segment, delivered as their management
data. Make clear they keep it whether or not anything follows.

## Touch 3 — the single-question close

One question, easy to answer either way. Then stop.

## InMail variant

Same structure, one message. Lead with the compliance framing rather than the revenue
framing — for this buyer, the CMS AI Exposure Review converts better than a product demo.

---

## Guardrails

- **Never mention month-to-month pricing.** That is the ICP-1 offer.
- **Never mention procurement-free or no-demo-gate.** Also ICP-1.
- **Security posture is stated honestly, early.** SOC 2 is not held; Coverage Voice has
  it. Raising it before they find it is the difference between a gap and a credibility
  problem.
- **Never claim multi-agency deployment is unique** (OF-2).
- **No price.** OF-1.
- The Exposure Review is a genuine audit. If it is offered, its findings get delivered
  even where they favour a competitor.
- Run `ops/QA-checklist.md` before first send.
