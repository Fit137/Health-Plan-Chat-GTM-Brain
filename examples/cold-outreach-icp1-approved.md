# Approved example — cold outreach touch 1, ICP-1

**The voice reference.** When a draft does not sound right, compare it against this. Every
line below has passed the claim ledger.

---

**To:** Agency owner, 6 licensed agents, Pinellas County FL
**Subject:** Sunday, 8:40pm, dental allowance

> I called your main line on Sunday at 8:40 in the evening and asked what the dental
> allowance is on the Aetna Medicare Eagle plan. It went to voicemail.
>
> I asked four other agencies in Pinellas the same question that evening. Three of the
> five went to voicemail too.
>
> I recorded all of it. Want the rest of what your line did with the other nineteen
> questions?
>
> — [Name]

---

## Why this works

**It opens with a fact, not a greeting.** The first six words are what happened. Nothing
is spent earning the right to say it.

**The specifics are checkable.** A named plan, a named county, a timestamp. The reader can
verify every element, which is what makes the fourth line credible.

**It never mentions the product.** No "AI", no company name in the body, no capability
claim. Remove the sender and the email still makes sense — but it also becomes obviously
worth answering, which is the test.

**The consequence is implied, not argued.** "Three of the five went to voicemail too" does
the work that a paragraph about missed-call economics would do worse. The reader draws the
conclusion, so they own it.

**The ask is the smallest possible.** Not a meeting. Not a demo. Not a calendar link. Just
permission to send something that already exists. That is the whole reason the reply rate
target is 8% rather than 2%.

**It survives the claim ledger.** No price. No capability. No customer reference. No
superlative. Nothing that needs a status flag.

---

## What was cut from the draft, and why

- *"We help Medicare agencies capture more of their inbound calls."* — describes the
  product, says nothing about the reader.
- *"Our AI agent can answer these questions 24/7."* — a capability claim in touch one,
  and it puts us in the receptionist category the positioning is trying to escape.
- *"Most agencies lose $2,082 per missed call."* — a modelled number presented as fact,
  and it belongs in touch three where it has been earned.
- *"Would you be open to a quick 15-minute call?"* — the ask is too large for a first
  touch and it opens with the demo gate.
