# ICP and buying committee

*Load this before writing to a specific audience, or when deciding which track a prospect
belongs on. Distils `sources/extracted/icp-dashboard.md`.*

## The two ICPs

**ICP-1 was chosen for one reason above all others: it is the segment careCycle's
$10,000/month floor has left unsold.** It has real inbound volume, real plan complexity,
and no credible offer today.

**ICP-2 is where the corpus advantage compounds** — one FMO relationship becomes
distribution across a downline.

| | **ICP-1 · MVP → PMF** | **ICP-2 · Growth** |
|---|---|---|
| Segment | Independent Medicare agencies with their own inbound number and website | Established agencies, General Agencies, FMO downlines |
| Location | US, single-state or 2–3 contiguous. Densest: FL, TX, AZ, CA, PA, OH, NC, MI | US multi-state, 5+. Bilingual markets disproportionately valuable |
| Revenue | $400K – $2.5M annual commission | $2.5M – $25M commission plus overrides |
| Team | 3–10 licensed agents, 1–2 admin. Often no marketing or ops hire | 10–50 in-house, 50–500 contracted downline. Has ops, compliance, marketing |
| Maturity | 3–10 years. Systematised on paper only. Growth capped by the owner's capacity | 8+ years. Multi-carrier, actively recruiting downline |
| Engagement model | **Low-touch, self-serve-led.** Published price, no demo gate. Month-to-month | **Assisted, annual.** Security review, pilot on one segment, then rollout |
| Decision | **One person.** The owner, usually also the top producer | **A committee.** Ops evaluates, compliance clears, the principal signs |

## The pain, stated the way they would state it

**ICP-1.** Calls go unanswered after hours and during AEP peak — a missed call is roughly
$2,082 in five-year member value. The five-minute lead window is routinely lost. The front
desk cannot answer plan-specific questions, so every "what's my dental allowance?" becomes
a callback or a lost caller. Cannot staff bilingual. The owner is the bottleneck on plan
knowledge, and handles compliance manually and anxiously.

**ICP-2.** All of the above at scale, plus: inconsistent answer quality across a downline
nobody can supervise call-by-call. Agent turnover destroys accumulated plan knowledge. CMS
exposure multiplied across every downline agent. No way to enforce disclaimer, SOA capture
or recording uniformly. The recruiting pitch needs a technology differentiator.

## Buying committee — ICP-1

| | Level 1 · User | Level 2 · Manager | Level 3 · Decision Maker |
|---|---|---|---|
| Titles | Licensed Medicare Agent, Front Desk, Client Services | Sales Manager, Agency Manager, Operations Lead | Agency Owner, Principal Broker, Managing Partner |
| Influence | **Low formal, high veto.** Cannot buy, but abandons anything that adds friction | Medium. Shapes the shortlist, runs the trial | **High — signs.** Usually also the top producer |
| Blocks because | "It'll answer wrong and I'll get blamed." Fear of replacement | Cannot afford a failed AEP experiment. No bandwidth Sept–Dec | Price against uncertain ROI. CMS liability. Burned by past tech |
| Wins because | Fewer repeat questions. Warm handoffs, not cold callbacks. Not working evenings | Coverage without a hiring req. Predictable Monday mornings | More policies on the same spend. Less personal dependence |
| Reached via | Facebook agent groups, Insurance Forums, FMO webinars | FMO newsletters, agent podcasts, AEP prep webinars, LinkedIn | Direct outbound with the free audit attached, FMO referral, peer word of mouth |

## Buying committee — ICP-2

Same three levels, different people and a real procurement process.

- **Level 1** is the contracted downline agent. They did not choose the tool and hold
  effective veto by quietly not using it. **Contractor adoption is the failure mode.**
  Agent comms addressing "is this replacing me" are a rollout deliverable, not an
  afterthought.
- **Level 2** is the Director of Operations, and is **frequently the true buyer**. The
  evaluation, the security review and the rollout plan all sit here. Sell here first.
- **Level 3** is the principal or FMO owner. They sign, and they care about the recruiting
  differentiator more than the operational detail.

## Design partner profile

The agency worth recruiting first: 5–8 licensed agents, one or two states, sells three or
more MA plans from at least two carriers, has its own inbound number, measurable
after-hours call loss, an owner who answers hard plan questions personally, and — the
binding filter — **available between February and mid-September**. From 15 October to
7 December they are unreachable at any price.

## The rule that must not be broken

An asset serves ICP-1 or ICP-2, never both. The buyers, channels, objections and
commercial terms differ entirely. The segmentation signal is which value driver they
completed: **D1 or D2 implies ICP-1; D5 or D6 implies ICP-2.**

An ICP-1 asset must not mention procurement or security review. An ICP-2 asset must not
mention month-to-month pricing.
