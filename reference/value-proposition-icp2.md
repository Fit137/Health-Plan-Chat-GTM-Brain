# Value proposition — ICP-2, the GA and FMO downline

*Load this before writing any ICP-2 asset. Never blend with ICP-1 material.*

## The promise

Consistent, compliant plan expertise across every agent in your downline.

One plan corpus, enforced uniformly. Every agent's front line answers to the same
standard, with disclaimer, escalation and recording built in. A recruiting asset, not just
an operations tool.

## Why the ICP-1 pitch does not transfer

ICP-1 buys relief from a personal bottleneck: the owner is the only person who knows the
plans, and they are tired. ICP-2 has no such bottleneck — it has 200 contracted agents,
each of whom is a separate compliance exposure the principal owns and cannot supervise
call by call.

So the ICP-1 frame ("it knows your plans") lands as a nice-to-have here. The frame that
lands is **enforceability**: this is the only control that makes answer quality uniform
across people you do not employ.

## Three pillars

### 1 · Uniform answer quality across people you cannot supervise

Agent turnover destroys accumulated plan knowledge; the corpus survives it. One ingestion
serves every agency in the downline selling that plan in that county. The variance between
your best agent and your newest one stops being a variable you manage.

### 2 · A compliance posture that is auditable, not aspirational

TPMO disclaimer, automated-system disclosure and recording with CMS ten-year retention,
applied identically on every call in the downline, whether or not the agent remembers.
Escalation to a licensed agent is bounded by design. Segment-level reporting shows what
was said and where it came from.

**State the gaps here, not later.** SOC 2 is not held. SOA capture is roadmap. Coverage
Voice has both. A compliance buyer will find this in week one of the review; finding it
from us in week one instead is the difference between a gap and a credibility problem.

### 3 · A recruiting differentiator the principal can use

Downline recruiting is a competitive market and technology is a live differentiator in it.
An FMO that can offer contracted agents a plan-fluent front line, co-branded, is offering
something its competitors are not. This is the argument that moves the principal, and it
is not an operations argument.

## Who to sell to, in order

1. **Director of Operations (Level 2)** — frequently the true buyer. The evaluation, the
   security review and the rollout plan all sit here. Start here.
2. **Compliance Officer** — a blocker who becomes an advocate if the exposure review is
   honest. D5 CMS AI Exposure Review converts better than a product demo for this buyer.
3. **Principal / FMO owner (Level 3)** — signs. Give them the recruiting angle.
4. **Contracted agents (Level 1)** — did not choose the tool, hold effective veto by
   quietly not using it. **Contractor adoption is the failure mode.** Agent comms
   addressing "is this replacing me" are a rollout deliverable.

## Commercial frame

Annual contract. Pilot on one downline segment with named success criteria, then phased
rollout with named expansion triggers. Per-agency or per-seat pricing, with expansion
pricing agreed up front.

**Never mention month-to-month.** That is the ICP-1 offer and raising it here invites a
procurement conversation about why the terms differ.

## Two things to never claim to this audience

- **That multi-agency deployment is unique to us.** It is not (OF-2). Coverage Voice ships
  white-labelled sub-accounts in 48 hours. Sell plan grounding *across* the downline —
  which they do not have — not the multi-tenancy, which they do.
- **Any security certification we do not hold.** This audience verifies.

## The motion with the widest reach

One FMO partnership reaches dozens of agencies at once. A downline-wide Gap Report is
management data the FMO wants whether or not they buy, which makes it the rare asset that
opens the relationship without asking for anything. Co-marketing then gives the FMO a
member benefit at no cost to them.

That is why ICP-2 is the growth motion and not the starting motion: it compounds, but it
requires a working reference from ICP-1 first.
