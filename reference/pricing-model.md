## How the price was set

Three-way triangulation, bottom-up.

**Cost floor.** A conversation costs about **$0.54** to serve — 3.5 minutes at $0.14
blended per minute for LLM, speech and telephony, plus $0.05 for recording, storage and
the CMS ten-year retention obligation. Fixed cost is about **$43** per account per month.
At an expected 180 conversations a month, cost to serve is $140, and at a 70% target gross
margin the floor is **$467**.

**Market band.** Normalised to per agency per month: Synthflow ~$66, Goodcall Scale $249,
Smith.ai Basic $600, careCycle $10,000. Low $66, median $424, high $10,000.

**That 150x spread is itself the finding. The category has no middle — and the middle is
exactly where a 3–10 agent agency lives.**

**Value ceiling.** Two incremental enrollments a month at the 2026 CMS initial commission
of $694 is $16,656 a year. Capturing 15% puts the ceiling at **$208 a month**.

## The proposed rate card

| | Front Desk | **Plan Fluent** | Downline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Role | Decoy | **Hero** | Anchor |
| Platform fee | $199 | **$499** | From $1,499 |
| Annual, per month | $169 | **$424** | Custom |
| Conversations included | 65 | **220** | 750 |
| Monthly overage ceiling | $299 | **$749** | Custom |
| **Worst-case bill** | **$498** | **$1,248** | Custom |

Overage bands above the allowance: first 250 at $1.45, next 750 at $1.15, next 2,000 at
$0.95, beyond at $0.80. Ten percent off with prepaid commit. **Surge cap: overage cannot
exceed 1.5x the platform fee in any month.**

The worst-case row is the number for a nervous buyer, and it is the entire point of the
surge cap: the worst possible invoice is knowable in advance.

## Why the packaging is shaped this way

Tiers were built from the feature matrix, not chosen first. All six differentiators are
L3 plan IP and no commodity feature reaches the differentiator zone, so **the Hero is
built on plan intelligence and nothing else.**

- **Front Desk (Decoy)** carries the 16 table-stakes features. Its job is to make Plan
  Fluent the obvious choice. Not designed to be a destination.
- **Plan Fluent (Hero)** adds the six plan-intelligence differentiators. Target 60–80% of
  ICP-1 here.
- **Downline (Anchor)** adds the niche features and custom terms. Anchors price high.

$499 clears the $467 floor at expected usage, sits above the commodity receptionist band,
and lands **20x below careCycle** — the OF-1 wedge expressed as a number.

## Two tensions stated rather than buried

**1. A generic 80% software gross margin is not reachable here.** Voice AI carries real
variable COGS. Holding 80% would force the Plan Fluent allowance down to roughly 105
conversations, which puts a typical agency into overage every month and breaks the
predictability the positioning rests on. The model targets 70% and accepts ~68% at full
allowance. Both are editable inputs.

**2. The value ceiling ($208) sits below the cost floor ($467).** Expected at MVP: the
value case rests on unconfirmed enrollment assumptions, so price currently leans on cost
and market, not value. **This is the number design partner data should replace first, and
the one that would justify raising price.**

## The workbook

Nine tabs. **All inputs live on the Control Panel; every other tab reads from it.** Blue
cells are editable inputs, white are formulas, green are key outputs, amber are
assumptions needing confirmation. Red fill anywhere means gross margin has fallen below
the floor set in Control Panel B52.
