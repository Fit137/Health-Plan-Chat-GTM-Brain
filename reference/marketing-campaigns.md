# Campaigns and asset plan

*Load this when planning a campaign or deciding which asset a stage needs. Distils
`sources/extracted/assets-library.md`. Channel method is in `outbound-engine.md`.*

## The demand drivers

Six, on a sliding scale of perceived value against ease of delivery. D3 is the spine.

| # | Driver | Perceived value | Delivery cost | Where it runs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Medicare AI Readiness Scorecard | Medium | Very low | Communities, forums |
| D2 | Missed-Call Revenue Calculator | Medium-high | Low | Paid traffic — the instant-payoff driver |
| **D3** | **Benefit Answer Gap Report** | **8.6** | **6.7** | **Everywhere. The TOFU spine of both funnels** |

What D3 actually is, and how it is run: `reference/gap-report-method.md`.
| D4 | 72-Hour Plan Brain Sandbox | Very high | High | Stage 3, after qualification |
| D5 | CMS AI Exposure Review | High | Medium | ICP-2 entry, and ICP-1 stage 4 |
| D6 | AEP Peak-Load Stress Test | High | Very high | Industry events only |

**The driver changes shape by channel, and this is the part people get wrong:**

| Channel | Form | Why this form here |
|---|---|---|
| Personalised cold outreach | Full report, **pre-run before contact** | The only format where the asset arrives before the ask. Highest conversion, highest cost per prospect |
| LinkedIn outreach | Offered, run on request | Reciprocity works cold on LinkedIn; running it unprompted at ICP-2 scale is not economic |
| LinkedIn ads | **D2 calculator** instead | A report takes 48 hours. Paid traffic needs an instant payoff |
| Communities and forums | **D1 scorecard** | Communities reject gated assets. D1 gives value before asking for anything |
| AEP webinar | Aggregate findings as content, individual report as the CTA | Anonymised aggregate data is the substance; the individual report is the conversion event |
| FMO co-marketing | Downline-wide report, co-branded | Converts one relationship into distribution; the aggregate is the FMO's own management data |

## The seven campaigns

**ICP-1**

- **A1 ★ Personalised cold outreach.** The highest-conversion motion. One Gap finding plus
  a recording of their own line, sent before any ask. Founder-led. Sourcing spec:
  `reference/job-signal-search.md`.
- **A2 Agent communities and forums.** Facebook agent groups, Insurance Forums,
  agent-only Slack and Discord. D1 scorecard, ungated, vendor affiliation disclosed.
- **A3 AEP prep webinar.** Aggregate audit findings as the substance.

**ICP-2**

- **B1 ★ LinkedIn outreach.** Directors of Ops, Compliance Officers, FMO principals — not
  agency owners. Under 300 characters per touch, no attachment on the first. Title-verify
  every prospect; ICP-1 owners get routed to A1 instead.
- **B2 ★ LinkedIn ads.** D2 calculator behind the ad. Target by title and company size.
  **Never target independent agency owners here.**
- **B3 FMO and carrier co-marketing.** The motion with the widest reach in the library: one
  relationship becomes distribution across dozens of agencies. The FMO must get standalone
  value even if nobody buys.

**Shared**

- **S1 Website and email nurture.** Two separate sequences, six for ICP-1 and eight for
  ICP-2. **Never merge the tracks.** Segmentation signal is which driver they completed.

## Build order, given the calendar

The AEP calendar decides sequence, not preference. Recruitment runs February to
mid-September; from 15 October to 7 December owners are unreachable.

1. **D3 Gap Report machinery** — everything else routes into it.
2. **A1 cold outreach** — highest conversion, lowest dependency.
3. **Website and pricing page** — blocked on OF-1, and OF-1 is the largest lever we hold.
4. **D4 sandbox** — the stage-3 conversion event.
5. **A2 and A3** — community presence compounds slowly, so start early.
6. **B1, B2** — ICP-2 needs an ICP-1 reference first.
7. **B3** — requires a partner agreement and a working reference.

## Standing QA gates

- **Minimum sample of 15 agencies before publishing any benchmark.** Aggregate audit data
  is the only proprietary asset the marketing engine has; publishing it thin destroys it.
- **No individual agency named in aggregate material without consent.**
- **No carrier or plan named in a way that implies endorsement.** CMS marketing rules
  apply to our customers and reflect on us.
- **The CMS AI Exposure Review is a genuine audit, not a disguised demo.** Deliver
  findings even where they favour a competitor. This is the sharpest
  problem-identification play we have and it only works if it is real.
- **Every comparison page states the competitor's genuine strength first**, accurately.

## Channels this GTM does not run

Consumer-facing advertising. Anything targeted at Medicare beneficiaries. Paid search on
beneficiary terms. Cold calling agencies. Generic mass email.

If asked for one of these, say the GTM excludes it and offer the nearest channel in the
mix. Do not produce a softened version.
