# Do not say — the claim ledger

Load with every writing task. **Every prohibition here carries a replacement.** If you
find yourself wanting a form that is not listed, it does not exist yet — raise it, do not
improvise one.

`last_reviewed: 2026-08-25`

## 1. Hard bans

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "SOC 2 certified", "SOC 2 compliant" | "SOC 2 is on our roadmap and not yet held. Here is our current security posture:" — then the real posture |
| "HIPAA compliant", "HIPAA certified" | "HIPAA-aligned handling" — and only alongside the specifics: encryption, retention, BAA availability |
| "Medicare-approved", "CMS-approved", "endorsed by Medicare" | "Built to CMS marketing rules for TPMOs" |
| Any price, any tier name, any figure | Nothing, until the price is published. Then: "Pricing is published at [URL]" |
| "Books the appointment", "schedules the appointment" | "Hands the qualified caller to your licensed agent with the whole conversation attached" |
| "Captures Scope of Appointment", "handles SOA" | Nothing. It is roadmap. If asked: "Not yet — it is on the pre-AEP roadmap" |
| "Provider lookup", "checks if their doctor is in network" | Nothing. UNVERIFIED (Q-3) |
| "Designed for older adults", "accessible by design" | Nothing. UNVERIFIED (Q-3) |
| "Never wrong", "100% accurate", "guaranteed accurate" | "Every answer resolves against the carrier's own document, and anything plan-specific escalates to your licensed agent" |
| "Replaces your front desk", "replaces an agent" | "Covers the hours and the questions your team cannot" |
| "Trusted by agencies across the country", any customer count, any logo, any testimonial | Nothing. There are no customers yet. Do not imply otherwise, including with vague plurals |
| "Enrols the beneficiary" | "Hands to your licensed agent, who writes the application" |
| "Instant setup", "live in minutes", "live in 48 hours" | "Live in five business days from the day we receive your plan documents" |

## 2. Channel and medium specific

**Video, webinar and any spoken script.** This is where status discipline breaks. The
TPMO disclaimer requirement, the escalation behaviour and the recording notice must be
stated, not implied, and cannot be cut for pacing. Never demonstrate a roadmap capability
on screen. Never let a demo call show an appointment being booked.

**Community and forum posts.** Agent communities reject gated assets and reject vendor
pitching. Give the value first and disclose that you are the vendor. A post that hides
the affiliation and gets caught costs the channel, not just the post.

**Paid social.** No beneficiary-facing targeting, ever. We market to agencies, not to
people on Medicare. Targeting seniors with anything that touches plan benefits is a CMS
marketing exposure for our customers and for us.

**Comparison pages.** State the competitor's genuine strength first and accurately. A
comparison page that reads as spin loses the reader at the first claim they can check.

## 3. Claims that need scoping

The drill: state the claim, name the counter-example, write the narrowed form. Keep the
counter-example — it is what stops the broad form creeping back.

### "The only AI that knows your Medicare plans"

- **Counter-example.** careCycle markets member-aware call handling with plan and benefit
  context. Whether it covers pre-sale benefit Q&A or only post-enrolment member servicing
  is **open (Q-1)** and unresolved.
- **Safe form.** "The only AI front line trained on the specific plans your agency sells,
  answering beneficiaries directly from the carrier's own documents — at a price an
  independent agency can carry."
- **Verdict.** Holds *with the price clause attached*. Drop the price clause and the claim
  is exposed to Q-1. Keep them together.

### "Only we can deploy across a downline"

- **Counter-example.** Coverage Voice explicitly markets sub-accounts across multiple
  agencies, white-labelled, live in 48 hours. Stated on their own TPA/BPO page.
- **Safe form.** "The only one that carries the same plan grounding across every agency in
  the downline."
- **Verdict.** The uniqueness claim is **falsified (OF-2)**. Never say we are the only
  multi-tenant option. Sell plan grounding across the downline, not the multi-tenancy.

### "Answers in your agency's name"

- **Counter-example.** Whether careCycle and Coverage Voice answer in the agency's name or
  their own is **open (Q-2)**. Coverage Voice markets white-labelling under the customer's
  own brand and domain, which suggests they do.
- **Safe form.** Describe what ours does. Do not assert that competitors cannot.
- **Verdict.** Scope to a statement about us until Q-2 is settled by a prospect call.

### "20x cheaper than the alternative"

- **Counter-example.** The careCycle figure is sourced from third-party pricing analysis,
  not from careCycle, who publish no pricing. Confidence is medium-high, not certain.
- **Safe form.** Internal only, for now. Externally, once price is published: "priced for
  an agency your size, not for a call centre."
- **Verdict.** Do not put a competitor's price in external material on a medium-high
  source. Confirm it via a prospect call first.

### "Scored 9.3 on plan-grounded answer depth"

- **Counter-example.** The three Medicare-native vendors have zero G2 and Capterra
  presence, so their scores rest on their own published material, not on review corpora.
- **Safe form.** Quote the score only with the method note attached, and only in material
  where the method can be read.
- **Verdict.** Internal and sales-enablement use. Not for an ad.

## 4. Historical and reputational material

There is no history to handle — Health Plan Chat is pre-revenue with no launched
predecessor. That is itself the thing to handle honestly. Do not manufacture heritage,
do not imply a track record, and do not use "we have seen" or "in our experience" about
customer outcomes we have not had.

**Competitor facts commonly misremembered.** Do not repeat these wrongly:

- Synthflow is horizontal and has no Medicare plan knowledge. It is not a Medicare
  product and saying it "does Medicare badly" is inaccurate.
- Infinitus is an outbound back-office agent that calls payers on behalf of providers. It
  does not do Medicare plan benefit Q&A for beneficiaries. A research pass claimed
  otherwise; it was wrong.
- SunFire is consumer-facing plan shopping, not an answering product.
- careCycle's weakness is price and segment focus, not capability. Saying their product is
  weak is both false and easy for a prospect to disprove.
