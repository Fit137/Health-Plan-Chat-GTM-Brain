**Update this file the moment status changes, before any copy follows.** Ledger first,
copy second, always. A claim about a capability is only as good as the flag next to it.

## Legend

| Flag | Means | How you may write about it |
|---|---|---|
| **SHIPPED** | Live now | Present tense, freely |
| **PARTIAL** | Partly live | Say what works today, never what it will do |
| **IN BUILD** | Committed, unshipped | "Coming soon", or omit. Never present tense |
| **ROADMAP** | Intent, uncommitted | Never a launch claim. Do not put a date on it |
| **UNVERIFIED** | Attributed to us by third-party research, not confirmed against the product | **Do not claim in any form** |

## The two that lose deals

**Appointment booking** and **SOA capture** are both ROADMAP, both table stakes, and both
sit in the same handoff sequence. Appointment booking scores +14.1 demand and both direct
competitors ship it. SOA is a hard CMS requirement for a TPMO and Coverage Voice captures
it in-call, at exactly the moment we hand off.

Shipping them wins nothing. Lacking them loses deals. Say "not yet, it is on the pre-AEP
roadmap" and move to what we do that they do not — never imply either exists.

## Demo rule

Nothing flagged IN BUILD, ROADMAP or UNVERIFIED may be screen-recorded, narrated in a
video, shown in a sandbox, or demonstrated live as though a customer could use it today.
Status discipline breaks first in demos and video, which is where it costs most.
